Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of the AVIF format?

AVIF is a next-generation image format that produces smaller files than JPG, PNG, and even WebP at the same visual quality. It's especially useful for optimizing website load times.

Is AVIF supported in all browsers?

AVIF display (decoding) is supported in the latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. The conversion itself runs entirely inside this tool in your browser, so it works even on older browsers.

Why is the first conversion slower?

The first conversion loads the AVIF encoder (about 1-2MB) once. Subsequent conversions are processed instantly.

Are files uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server, ensuring 100% privacy and data security. Safe for sensitive documents and enterprise security environments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Are passport and ID photo sizes different?

Yes. A passport photo is 3.5×4.5cm, a resume/ID photo is 3×4cm, and a business-card size photo is 5×7cm. Pick the preset you need and it's applied automatically.

Q. How do I use the print sheet?

Check "Make a print sheet" to arrange multiple copies of your photo on a single 4×6 inch print sheet, then print the downloaded file at a photo studio or convenience-store printer.

Q. Can I change the background to white?

To remove and replace the background, first use our AI Background Remover to make it transparent, then crop it to size here.
